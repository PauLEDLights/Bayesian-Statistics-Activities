https://machinelearningplus.com/predictive-modeling/how-naive-bayes-algorithm-works-with-example-and-full-code/

The objective of this example is to predict current human activity based on phisiological activity measurements from 53 different features based in the HAR dataset.
https://web.archive.org/web/20210417051205/http://groupware.les.inf.puc-rio.br/har#sbia_paper_section

The training and test datasets are provided.
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/selva86/datasets/master/har_train.csv
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/selva86/datasets/master/har_validate.csv

Build a Naive Bayes model, predict on the test dataset and compute the confusion matrix.
